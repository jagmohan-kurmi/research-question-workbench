# Research basis and limits

PICO supports question structuring in many quantitative health contexts, while SPIDER was developed for qualitative and mixed-methods evidence synthesis (Cooke, Smith & Booth, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1471-1842.2012.00896.x). FINER (feasible, interesting, novel, ethical, relevant) is a question-refinement heuristic described by Cummings, Browner & Hulley in *Designing Clinical Research* (4th ed., 2013). These structures are prompts, not universal requirements. EQUATOR distinguishes reporting guidance from making study-design decisions: https://www.equator-network.org/toolkits/writing-research/.

## Limits

The checker reports missing text and exact overlaps between user-entered inclusion and exclusion criteria only. It does not assess novelty, feasibility, ethics, relevance, scientific merit, or whether the chosen framework fits a discipline. Researchers must interpret every field and consult current institutional requirements.
